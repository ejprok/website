What is this website for?

Been thinking more about technology, ai, and the general state of the internet. With all the change there is also noise.

There is a sense of weariness that accompanies every online interaction.

Is what I'm seeing real?

Am I being sold something?

Have I chosen my own values or have they been chosen for me?

This website started as a portfolio site. A place to sell myself. I'm a bit sick of that though. A simple creative outlet, that's all this place is for now.

Exhaustion turned to frustration

I was browsing the internet on my phone recently. It's amazing how poor of an experience that has become. After 15 minutes, my phone was hot and my battery had been drained a good bit.

But the worst thing by far was the UX. Even with a fast and modern internet connection, most websites were slow, filled with popups, and if you interact too quickly the page might crash.

We already know why this is. Heavy use of flashy animations, unoptimized assets, and pointless scripts that don't benefit the user.

It can really feel like everything is turning into garbage. It isn't just websites, if you let yourself get down you'll see it everywhere.

Peace and quiet

I'm going to try to carve out a little piece of the internet that I've felt has been missing.

A place to stay a bit more positive, and maybe stay excited for what is to come.
